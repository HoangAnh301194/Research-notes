4.3     Long-term Video Understanding

Datasets and Settings. We rigorously assess VideoMamba’s proficiency in
processing long-term videos using three comprehensive datasets: Breakfast [38],
COIN [72], and Long-form Video Understanding (LVU [86]). Breakfast comprises
1,712 videos of 10 intricate cooking activities over 77 hours. COIN features 11,827
videos across 180 procedural tasks, averaging 2.36 minutes. The LVU benchmark
includes approximately 30K movie clips lasting 1 to 3 minutes, encompassing
nine tasks across three categories: content understanding, metadata prediction,
and user engagement. For regression tasks, we evaluate using mean-squared error;
for classification tasks, accuracy is the metric of choice. Unlike prior studies [36,
47] that rely on features from pretrained video models like Swin-B [51] trained
on Kinetics-600, our method uses end-to-end training as detailed in Section 4.2.
For fair comparisons, we fine-tune our models pretrained on K400.
